## NATURaL — Support

### Getting started

NATURaL is chair yoga you can begin immediately. There is no account and no
subscription, and every session is available from the first launch. Open the app,
choose a session, and follow along from a stable chair.

### Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. NATURaL works fully without one. If you have a paired Apple Watch and allow
Health access, the app can read heart rate variability during a session and adapt
what it shows you. That is optional.

### Health permissions

NATURaL asks for Health access only for the signals it uses during a session. You
can grant or refuse each type in the Health app, and change your mind later under
**Health → Sharing → Apps**. Refusing has no effect on session content — the
guidance, poses and timing are identical either way.

### Showing a session on a TV

During a session, the TV control in the toolbar offers a display for a nearby
Apple TV running NATURaL, or for AirPlay screen mirroring. The connection is made
on your own local network and is encrypted. Nothing is stored on the television.

### Where is my data?

On your device. NATURaL has no account system and no server. See the privacy
policy for detail.

### Accessibility

NATURaL supports Dynamic Type including the largest accessibility sizes, VoiceOver
and Reduce Motion. If you find a screen that does not work with your settings,
that is a bug worth reporting.

### Languages

English and French.

### Something isn't working

Contact: *[Support contact — see open gaps.]*

When reporting a problem, the most useful details are your device model, iOS
version, and what you were doing when it happened.

### It is not a medical device

NATURaL is a wellness app. Its entropy indicators do not diagnose conditions and
do not measure drug binding. Confirm anything health-related with your clinician.

# NATURaL privacy policy source and release review

Published: [thebonhomme.com/NATURaL/privacy/](https://thebonhomme.com/NATURaL/privacy/).

## Product rule

NATURaL analyses signals on the user’s device. We do not collect, receive or keep personal or health data. There is no NATURaL account, no developer analytics, and no iCloud or CloudKit sync in the shipping app.

The published English source is [site/NATURaL/privacy/index.html](site/NATURaL/privacy/index.html). All 11 translations are generated from [site-locales.json](site-locales.json) by `scripts/build-app-site.py`. Edit the locale source and regenerate, rather than maintaining a second policy text here.

App Privacy in App Store Connect: **Data Not Collected**. Required-reason API manifests describe on-device UserDefaults use and are not a data-collection questionnaire. Optional Apple Health, Apple Music, Watch, local display and SharePlay are Apple or device-to-device services the user chooses; they do not send records to Le Bonhomme Pharma.

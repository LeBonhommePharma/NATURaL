Privacy policy

Effective 15 September 2026. Applies to NATURaL 1.0. Developed by Le Bonhomme Pharma, Montréal, Québec. Contact: lp@thebonhomme.com.

Your data stays with you

We do not collect, receive or keep your personal or health data. NATURaL has no account, no developer analytics, and no iCloud or CloudKit sync. Sessions, preferences, recovery snapshots, prescribed activities and medication entries stay on your device so analysis can run locally. Health-storage directories are excluded from app backups. Removing the app removes its private local storage; shared App Group storage can remain while another app in the group is installed.

Apple Health and consent

You choose which Health data the app may read and write on your device, including available heart-rate, variability, activity, energy and workout samples. Clinical medication access has a separate consent flow. Guided sessions work without Health. We never receive those samples. Apple controls Health storage and any Health synchronization you enable. Manage access and delete Health records in Apple Health.

Optional Apple services you choose

If you play Apple Music, Apple processes authorization and playback under Apple’s privacy policy. Session features can relay information to your paired Watch, a connected local display or SharePlay participants you invite. Sharing a workout card creates a copy at your chosen destination. Recipients control their copies. This app does not load third-party web players or advertising.

Retention

We cannot read your on-device records. Local records remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app. Health records and exported copies must be removed separately. Earlier development builds may have written to an iCloud container this release no longer uses or accesses. This app does not delete those leftover records. You can remove them from iCloud settings for that Apple ID.

Website and contact

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and processes requests under GitHub’s privacy statement. This section adds no analytics, advertising pixels or contact forms. The shared theme control and language selector store your preferences in this browser. Email is processed by your and our mail services; do not send health records unless you choose to. Policy changes are dated above.

Movement first. Metrics optional.

Signal variation is influenced by movement, breathing and sampling. Experimental entropy indicators are not a diagnosis, proof of medication effects or measurements of molecular binding.

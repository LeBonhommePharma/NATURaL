Small movements. Your rhythm.

You don’t need a perfect moment. Just a place to begin.

01

Find your seat.

Choose a stable chair and space to move. Follow a guided sequence, with pose descriptions and breathing cues.

02

Move at your pace.

Adapt movements to your comfort. Pause, resume or finish early. Every plan is open from the start.

03

Keep your practice close.

Revisit sessions in your on-device history. Apple Health and the Watch companion are optional ways to accompany your practice.

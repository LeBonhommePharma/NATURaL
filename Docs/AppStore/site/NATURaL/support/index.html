Start a session

Use a stable chair with room to move. Choose a plan and tap Begin Session. Use Pause, Resume and End as needed. Adapt movements to your comfort. Requires iOS / iPadOS 18+ or watchOS 10+.

Health, history and saving

Choose Health permissions in About & Privacy. A missing metric can mean no permission, no sample or no compatible sensor. Local sessions appear in History. If saving fails, retry from the summary. If temporary storage is reported, keep the app open while resolving storage problems; temporary records do not survive closing it.

Report a problem

Email your app version, device model, OS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots or crash reports help; remove personal and health details. For Watch recording problems, check Health permissions and retry. A save-error message means you should not assume the workout reached Health.

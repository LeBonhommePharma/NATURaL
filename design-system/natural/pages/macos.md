# macOS — a place for guided movement

Overrides MASTER for `BonhommeMac/`. Added 19 September 2026 after LP expanded release scope to the existing native Mac target.

- **Welcome:** approved bloom, clear headline, plan picker, duration and pose count, one mint Begin button. Pose disclosure gives users enough detail to choose a comfortable plan.
- **Session:** pose instruction and countdown first. Elapsed time and real pose progress second. Pause/Resume and End always visible in the scrolling flow; Space controls pause, Return begins/finishes.
- **Data honesty:** no decorative SCI, heart-rate or music readout when Mac has no feed. Say where live Health readings are supported without implying an existing Mac connection.
- **Outcome:** ending early reports completed poses and time; full completion gets the completion headline. Closing the window cancels/resets the guided timer.
- **Window:** 480 × 420 minimum; 760-point readable column; scroll for small windows and large text. Native window management, controls and focus.
- **Identity:** approved bloom app icon exported to all native macOS slots; same ink, mint, violet and SF typography. Privacy/Support links at the foot of the window.
- **Validation:** source/parser and packaging checks can run without Xcode. Rendered appearance, keyboard/VoiceOver and actual resize behavior need a compiled app; currently pending.
